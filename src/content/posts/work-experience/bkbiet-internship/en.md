## Where and when

I completed my summer industrial training at B. K. Birla Institute of Engineering & Technology, BKBIET, in Pilani, Rajasthan, India. I worked with the Computer Science & Engineering department from 28 April to 13 June 2025.

## What I worked on

My main project was [Advisory](../../projects/advisory/en.md), an app connecting advisors and people seeking advice. The work included researching similar platforms, wireframes, development with Expo / React Native and Supabase, testing, and documentation.

The app has its own project page. Here I want to write about the internship, the people I worked with, and living in Pilani.

## A challenge I encountered

Unstable internet interrupted work that depended on APIs and cloud services. During outages, I worked on local development, design, and documentation, then used more reliable connection periods for communication.
