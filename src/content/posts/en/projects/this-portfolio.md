## This website

I forked [Kanade](https://github.com/sudoriaa/Kanade-Astro) and adapted it for my personal website.

It uses Astro, Vue, and Tailwind CSS. I changed the palette to match the L banner and added English / Thai pages, post search, nested tags, and media galleries.

The site is static. Search and filters run in the browser.
